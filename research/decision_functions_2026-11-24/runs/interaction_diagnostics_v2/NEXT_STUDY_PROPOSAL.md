## Question and sequence

First establish whether the fixed head retains useful interaction recovery at the target 16-support budget. Use nested support budgets 4, 16, 32 and 96, preserving equal observed cases/outcomes across B1/B2/B3/M1. Preserve matched people and whole rotational question groups. Predeclare a balanced 16-case support construction, its information matrix and ordering before generating new fixtures; 16 cannot be silently treated as a whole number of three-case rotational groups. Keep rank two, canonical rho=.01 and offset penalty .1 fixed for this first diagnostic, with no new hyperparameter search. Reuse v2 only as exposed development evidence. Freeze new generator/label seeds, exact solve counts, numerical checks and two independent confirmation realizations before execution.

Retain the four v2 predictive gates separately at primary k=16 on each fresh realization: expected-NLL gain >=.05, oracle-gap recovery >=.50, contrast NRMSE <=.50, null degradation <=.01. This is an intentionally demanding capability check, not a promise that the gates will pass. Secondary support budgets cannot rescue primary failure. Include uninformative features and matched-other histories under exactly equal information budgets; predeclare deterministic donor schedules and independently verify their use. Report accuracy/Brier and their tradeoffs rather than requiring NLL and accuracy to agree. Numerical ties remain 1e-5 NLL and require a deterministic rule; retain float64 objectives, loss clipping, batch invariance and immutable ledgers.

Only if limited-history numerical capability is established, prepare a separate representation protocol. Compare known informative numerical features against fixed text-derived features under identical people, questions, support outcomes and grouping. The representation contract must define how text expresses the planted question variables without using person identities, answers or oracle probabilities. Keep all paraphrases of one semantic question together and prevent template leakage across query groups. Fit normalization/centering on training questions only; freeze encoder/tokenizer revisions and preprocessing before fitting. Do not infer representation adequacy from synthetic wording variants that were never encoded.

The exact text generator, semantic-variable mapping, representation dimension, encoder admission, candidate count and resource ledger need review before this second experiment is executable. A poor text result must distinguish inaccessible signal from head failure using the matched numerical-feature control. Do not search representations on held-out results. Report per-person and per-question-group variation and use paired resampling that preserves person pairs and question groups; distinguish conditional query-label uncertainty from training/support-label and generated-realization uncertainty. Initialization seeds are not independent datasets.
